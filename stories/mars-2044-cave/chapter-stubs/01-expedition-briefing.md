## Chapter purpose

Show that the colony's first human survey beyond the prepared perimeter is as
much an audit of inherited machine judgement as a geology mission.

## POV and pressure

Stay close to Mei. She should feel the practical pressure from Nadiya and the
intellectual pressure from Idris at the same time.

## Continuity notes

- The colony needs eastward ground soon, which makes caution expensive.
- Idris should begin in defence of the Argosy fleet, not in opposition to Mei.
- End with a clean launch into the field rather than a bureaucratic linger.
