## Chapter purpose

Answer the first mystery, why the site vanished from the maps, and replace it
with a deeper one that the machines themselves could not explain.

## POV and pressure

Let Mei feel the floor shift under her assumptions. Idris should get the relief
of an explanation, then lose it when the object proves older than the fleet.

## Continuity notes

- The hold order should feel operationally credible.
- The inner object must be concrete, local, and materially specific.
- End with the clear knowledge that the machines were early witnesses, not
  originators.
