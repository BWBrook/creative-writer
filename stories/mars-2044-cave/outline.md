# Outline

## Premise

In 2044, the first substantial human colony on Mars begins local exploration of
terrain prepared over the previous decade by autonomous construction systems. A
small EVA team finds a cave whose interior contains signs that another
intelligence reached it first.

## Narrative arc

1. Establish the colony, the robot-built infrastructure, and the first survey
   mission beyond the immediate habitat perimeter.
2. Lead the survey team to a newly noticed cave entrance in basaltic terrain.
3. Shift from exploration confidence to careful uncertainty once the team
   observes artifacts or traces inconsistent with natural geology.
4. End on an interpretive turn that expands the story's mystery rather than
   fully resolving it.

## Open questions

- Is the prior intelligence alien, human, artificial, or still ambiguous?
- Which colonist becomes the interpretive anchor for the discovery?
