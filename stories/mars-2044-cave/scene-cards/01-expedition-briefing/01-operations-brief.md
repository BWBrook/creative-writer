# Operations Brief

## Scene purpose

Authorize the first serious off-perimeter survey and establish the colony's
dependence on the Argosy construction fleet.

## POV

Stay close to Mei so the meeting reads as a practical decision rather than a
speech scene.

## Pressure

Nadiya needs the corridor cleared quickly, while Idris wants to treat the
construction fleet's maps as trustworthy unless proven otherwise.

## Turn

The mission shifts from routine route clearance to an audit of inherited
machine judgement when Mei notices that the missing map strip aligns too neatly
with the planned eastward corridor.

## Exit state

The chapter should leave the colony behind cleanly, with the field mission now
carrying both operational urgency and intellectual doubt.

## Continuity hooks

- Keep Nadiya practical rather than antagonistic.
- Let Idris defend the fleet in good faith.
