# Ridge Approach

## Scene purpose

Move from normal EVA procedure into the first undeniable anomaly.

## POV

Mei should notice the ridge as a geologist first, with the mystery emerging
from material observation rather than genre signalling.

## Pressure

The team is still on a real EVA clock, with Nadiya's remote expectation that
the survey stay productive rather than turn exploratory.

## Turn

The cave becomes a story-worthy site when the scored basalt and hidden witness
marker prove that omission from the maps was deliberate.

## Exit state

The team has no honest way to treat the cave as routine terrain anymore and has
to move inward with sharper purpose.

## Continuity hooks

- The geology observations need to carry the suspense.
- Use the witness marker as the chapter's hard pivot.
