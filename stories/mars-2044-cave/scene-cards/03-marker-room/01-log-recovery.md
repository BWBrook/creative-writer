# Log Recovery

## Scene purpose

Explain the map omission without dissolving the deeper mystery.

## POV

Stay with Mei as relief and unease arrive almost together.

## Pressure

Idris wants the recovered machine behaviour to restore trust in the fleet, but
the evidence has to stay specific enough that it can still surprise them.

## Turn

The construction fleet is cleared of concealment-by-error and recast as the
first witness to an older manufactured object.

## Exit state

The chapter should close with the first mystery resolved and a larger one now
fully unavoidable.

## Continuity hooks

- Make the machine hold order specific and credible.
- The chamber object should feel local to Martian basalt, not like a dropped
  piece of shiny science fiction.
