# Hold Order

## Scene purpose

Force Mei to make the colony's first ethical decision about a site it does not
understand.

## POV

Keep Mei close enough that the final choice feels like judgement under pressure
rather than a symbolic declaration.

## Pressure

The expedition has enough evidence to justify caution but not enough knowledge
to explain what the site is, which makes delay and action equally costly.

## Turn

The story ends when Mei chooses preservation and a fresh unexplained score keeps
the chamber open in the reader's mind.

## Exit state

The colony has a decision it can act on, while the cave remains intellectually
open.

## Continuity hooks

- Keep the ending physical and restrained.
- Avoid a speech about first contact.
