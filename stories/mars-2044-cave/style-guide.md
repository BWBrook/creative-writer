# Style Guide

## Intent

This story should read as near-future hard science fiction with a lean, serious
prose style and a buried first-contact mystery that remains disciplined all the
way through.

## Narrative anchor

- Stay in close third person around Mei Zhang.
- Let Idris Vale function as the counter-mind: more trusting of the machines,
  quicker to seek an explanation, slower to treat omission as malice.
- Keep Commander Nadiya Sorel mostly on comms so the colony's practical
  pressures stay present without diffusing the point of view.

## Voice and tone

- Favor procedural realism over spectacle.
- Keep the prose restrained, concrete, and economical.
- Let tension arise from discovery, uncertainty, and interpretation.
- Make the colony feel inhabited by work, limits, and inherited machine labour.

## Preferred qualities

- Scientific plausibility
- Clear scene purpose
- Sensory detail used sparingly and precisely
- Mystery built through clues rather than melodrama
- Dialogue that sounds like competent people thinking under pressure
- Chapter endings that change the reader's interpretation of the site

## Avoid

- Florid description for its own sake
- Empty cosmic awe language
- Obvious AI crutches such as repetitive em dashes or contrastive flourishes
- Villain energy where the story really needs uncertainty
- Exposition blocks that explain the science before the characters need it
- An ending that resolves the maker of the cave object too neatly
