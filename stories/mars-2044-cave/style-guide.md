# Style Guide

## Intent

This story should read as near-future hard science fiction with a lean, serious
prose style.

## Voice and tone

- Favor procedural realism over spectacle.
- Keep the prose restrained, concrete, and economical.
- Let tension arise from discovery, uncertainty, and interpretation.

## Preferred qualities

- Scientific plausibility
- Clear scene purpose
- Sensory detail used sparingly and precisely
- Mystery built through clues rather than melodrama

## Avoid

- Florid description for its own sake
- Empty cosmic awe language
- Obvious AI crutches such as repetitive em dashes or contrastive flourishes
