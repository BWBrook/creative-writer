# World Notes

- Ares Colony was assembled over roughly a decade by the Argosy construction
  fleet before the first substantial human occupation wave arrived.
- The story takes place on Sol 11 of that occupation wave. The colony is real,
  functional, and still provisional.
- The eastward expansion corridor matters materially: new shielding mass and
  ice-adjacent excavation ground would improve the colony's survival margin
  before the next supply window.
- EVA logistics, suit consumables, rover range, and local comms should stay
  plausible for 2044 technology.
- The cave is a lava-tube branch in basaltic terrain. Any cold-trap frost,
  salt-bearing seep, or possible biosignature must remain physically modest and
  geologically grounded.
- The story's first intelligence inside the cave can remain unresolved, but the
  evidence must feel concrete enough that cautious professionals would halt
  expansion.
- Earth exists mostly as distant authority. The real decisions in this story
  happen on Mars, inside a short operational window.
