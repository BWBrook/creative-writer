# Outline

This is the combined synopsis and structural plan for `Secrets of Sokol Keep`.

## Working premise

The beacon at Sokol Keep has gone dark, and the docks of Phlan are already
turning anxious and angry. Keria, a mercenary who once drank with Guard Sergeant
Grim Thornbuck, is drawn into the matter when rumour, brawl, and official
convenience all point toward Thorn Island.

On the island, the absence of the Black Fist garrison gives way to a quieter
truth: Igan Sokol found an old shrine and a wet temple below the keep, judged
the jade idol within to be evil, and was murdered by Grim for refusing its
treasure. Harae, the dead priestess bound to the forgotten shrine, has put out
the beacon so the living will recover the body and make the crime known.

## Structural plan

1. The docks establish public pressure: the beacon is dark, trade is delayed,
   and everyone is ready to blame someone else.
2. Keria and Aravele obtain enough sanction from House Sokol and the Black Fist
   to cross to Thorn Island.
3. The keep investigation turns haunting into evidence: servants, rooms,
   journal, shrine, and slab all narrow the path.
4. The descent into the Jade Temple converts rumour into danger and reveals
   that the garrison entered below.
5. Igan's body and Grim's loot collapse the false account.
6. The chosen justice is public custody and testimony, allowing the beacon to
   burn again without pretending the dead have been satisfied cheaply.
