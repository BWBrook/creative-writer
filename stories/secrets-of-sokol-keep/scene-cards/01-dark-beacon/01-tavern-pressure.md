# Tavern Pressure

## Scene purpose

Turn the dark beacon from background trouble into a public confrontation that
pulls Keria into action.

## POV

Keria. She reads rooms professionally and is slow to judge Grim because she
knows him.

## Pressure

Dockworkers need someone to blame, Keria and Aravele are visibly connected to
the missing Black Fist detail, and the tavern could become violent.

## Turn

Thirus's anger becomes useful testimony: the servants were seen alive, the
guards and Igan were not.

## Exit state

Keria and Aravele decide to seek permission in the morning.

## Continuity hooks

- Markoth responds to being called by his real name.
- Imizael establishes the line against blades.
- Grim's debts and treasure talk enter the record early.
