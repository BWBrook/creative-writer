# Official Threshold

## Scene purpose

Make House Sokol and the Black Fist both complicit in delay while granting Keria
enough authority to cross.

## POV

Keria, with Aravele as sharper social pressure.

## Pressure

Liela wants control, Hurn wants the problem solved cheaply, and Karst treats
the island as old trouble rather than a simple errand.

## Turn

The investigation becomes sanctioned and physically crosses from rumour to site.

## Exit state

Keria reaches Thorn Island and sees the keep's maintained but abandoned quiet.

## Continuity hooks

- Liela records and protects House Sokol.
- Hurn asks for proof that will stand.
- Karst's island stories frame the haunting without confirming it.
