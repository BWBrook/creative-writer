# Harae Guides

## Scene purpose

Convert haunting into evidence through Igan's room, the shrine, journal, and
West Tower slab.

## POV

Keria. She notices weight, cold, moved objects, and the difference between theft
and evidence.

## Pressure

The servants fear both the missing soldiers and the presence in the keep.
Keria must respect the dead enough for Harae to help.

## Turn

The story gains the phrase, location, and physical entrance to the temple below.

## Exit state

The slab opens and the investigation descends.

## Continuity hooks

- Harae answers through small actions.
- Igan's piety and obsession remain intertwined.
- Taking House Sokol treasure would damage Harae's trust.
