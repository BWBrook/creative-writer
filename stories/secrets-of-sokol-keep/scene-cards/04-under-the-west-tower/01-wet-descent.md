# Wet Descent

## Scene purpose

Make the under-temple dangerous through physical environment and reveal the cut
rope as evidence of human betrayal.

## POV

Keria, focusing on footing, rope, light, pain, and tracks.

## Pressure

Cold, mud, old guardians, and limited light make the descent costly.

## Turn

The cut rope and Black Fist cloth prove the guards came below and did not leave
cleanly.

## Exit state

Keria and Aravele push through toward the idol chamber.

## Continuity hooks

- The guardian armour is a hazard to solve, not a fight to glorify.
- Keria's shoulder injury carries forward.
- The temple's old cult remains ambiguous.
