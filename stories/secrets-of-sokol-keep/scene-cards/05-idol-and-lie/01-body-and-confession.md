# Body and Confession

## Scene purpose

Use Igan's wound, the stolen reliquary objects, and the guards' fear to collapse
Grim's lie.

## POV

Keria. Her knowledge of Grim makes the evidence harder and more necessary.

## Pressure

The idol chamber is physically dangerous, and Grim still has authority over men
who may keep lying.

## Turn

The murder becomes provable and Keria chooses custody instead of killing Grim.

## Exit state

The party has body, prisoners, relics, and testimony to carry back.

## Continuity hooks

- Igan's corpse is tragic evidence.
- Grim is pathetic and dangerous in the same scene.
- Harae's pressure should be felt through cold and attention, not speech.
