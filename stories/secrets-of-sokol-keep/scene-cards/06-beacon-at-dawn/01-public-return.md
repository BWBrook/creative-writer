# Public Return

## Scene purpose

Resolve the dark beacon through public evidence, custody, and Harae's acceptance.

## POV

Keria, tired and practical.

## Pressure

Institutions will try to own the account, Grim still has rank, and the harbour
is watching.

## Turn

The evidence is entered into public hands and the beacon relights.

## Exit state

The harbour resumes work with the murder known and Igan's body ready for proper
care.

## Continuity hooks

- Aravele keeps a copy of the truth because neither institution deserves all of
  it.
- Harae's acceptance appears as restored light.
- The ending closes on work rather than celebration.
