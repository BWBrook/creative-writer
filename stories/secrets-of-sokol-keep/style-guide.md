# Style Guide

Use `style-card.md` as the inherited register: haunted investigation, restrained
prose, practical adults, law and mercy under pressure. This file tunes that
card for a Sinew & Steel narrative conversion of the Sokol Keep module.

## Voice and tone

- Close third past, primarily through Keria.
- Plain, physical prose with a dockside and sea-weather texture.
- Haunting should feel evidentiary rather than theatrical.
- Keep emotion in action: rowing, searching, carrying, locking, washing, waiting.

## Things to lean toward

- Clues as objects and contradictions.
- Institutional pressure between House Sokol and the Black Fist.
- Harae as a dead witness who corrects the living by arranging facts.
- Grim as weak, greedy, frightened, and guilty rather than grandly evil.
- Justice as a practical act that must survive public scrutiny.

## Things to avoid

- Game mechanics, boxed text, or room-by-room transcription.
- Modern banter or self-aware adventuring language.
- Over-explaining the cult, the idol, or the haunting.
- Treating violence as spectacle when the stronger tension is evidentiary.
- Letting the dungeon swallow the murder mystery.
