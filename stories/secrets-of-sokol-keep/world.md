# World Notes

Optional artifact for setting, history, and world-specific constraints.

## Established facts

- Phlan's docks depend on the Sokol Keep beacon for trade confidence and coastal
  warning.
- Thorn Island sits in the Bay of Phlan as a breakwater, weathered by salt wind,
  reefs, hard currents, and ferry traffic.
- Sokol Keep was once a temple to Tyr, ruined during the Dragon Run, later
  restored and partly domesticated by House Sokol.
- House Sokol treats the keep as property and revenue; the Black Fist treats it
  as a duty post and punishment detail.
- The East Tower hides a restored shrine to Tyr tended by Igan Sokol and watched
  by Harae.
- The West Tower conceals the entrance to the Jade Temple, an older wet shrine
  associated ambiguously with Dagon or a Moonsea cult of "the Just One and the
  Great One."
- Harae can move small objects, create knocks or cold air, and arrange evidence,
  but she cannot simply speak the truth.

## Adaptation constraints

- Keep Sinew & Steel assumptions fiction-forward: uncertainty, fatigue, light,
  position, and consequence matter, but no rule language belongs in the prose.
- The story is a private-use pastiche from a D&D-derived module source.
- The chosen narrative path favours investigation and public justice over
  treasure-taking or private execution.
