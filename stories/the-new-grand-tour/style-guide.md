# Style Guide

This is a core artifact for `The New Grand Tour`.

## Voice and tone

- First-person travel journalism from a technically literate, observant narrator.
- Lean, confident prose that treats engineering detail as legible and interesting.
- Wonder should arise from scale, competence, and lived-in normality rather than melodrama.

## Things to lean toward

- Clear sequencing of itinerary, logistics, and built environment.
- Concrete details that make future space travel feel ordinary enough to be inhabited.
- Social observation: who lives here, who visits, and what daily life looks like.
- A tone that can be wry or lightly amused without becoming flippant.

## Things to avoid

- Generic “sense of wonder” language untethered from material details.
- Glossy futurism that hides infrastructure instead of revealing it.
- Sudden lapses into action-thriller pacing or melodramatic peril.
- Repetitive AI tics such as needless em dashes, contrastive flourishes, or vague uplift.
