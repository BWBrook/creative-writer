# World Notes

## Setting frame

- The story takes place in 2052, in a world where cislunar travel is expensive
  but recognisably commercial.
- The California-Moon-L5 route is structured enough to feel like a premium
  travel circuit rather than a one-off stunt.
- The post-2035 automation surge is a foundational background fact for orbit,
  the Moon, and farther-out construction.

## Cislunar infrastructure

- Launch from Earth is still physically intense, but operationally routine.
- Concord Gateway acts as an orbital transfer ring and staging hotel.
- Lunar water extraction at the south pole powers a large share of the cislunar
  economy.
- Freight can move by electromagnetic launch systems, while passengers remain on
  more conservative high-thrust craft.

## O’Neill-1 facts to preserve

- O’Neill-1 sits at Earth-Moon L5.
- Approximate geometry: 500 metres in radius, 8 kilometres long, about
  25 square kilometres of habitable inner surface.
- Rotation rate is about 1.34 rpm.
- Population is a little above forty-two thousand residents plus several
  thousand transients.
- The city remains compact, purposeful, and visibly engineered rather than
  pastoral or faux-rustic.

## Social and tonal truths

- The Moon and L5 are no longer defined solely by danger or heroism; they are
  increasingly ordinary, inhabited, and politically/socially textured.
- Tourism is real, but labour remains visible everywhere beneath it.
- Luxury exists in orbit, yet it still sits adjacent to infrastructure,
  engineering, and work.
- “City break at L5” is a newly plausible phrase, which captures the setting’s
  social shift better than technical exposition alone.

## Sequel opportunities

- A longer residence at Shephard could foreground workers, planners, school
  life, and extraction politics rather than first-visit wonder.
- A return to O’Neill-1 could follow governance, neighbourhood identity, rent,
  district prestige, or industrial conflict.
- The narrator could continue onward to Mars, using the California-Moon-L5
  route as an established prelude to deeper expansion.
- The Dyson-swarm rumours create a natural pathway for stories about solar
  industry, speculation, and outward-moving machine ecologies.
