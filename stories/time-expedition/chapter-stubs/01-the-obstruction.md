## Chapter purpose

Reopen the source-canon frame, reconcile the delayed return without solving the
temporal obstruction, and convert a scientific uncertainty into committed
backward travel.

## POV and pressure

The anonymous narrator controls the scene. Filby presses the Traveller away
from retrospective explanation and towards a new test; the Traveller's
curiosity, vanity, and appetite for adventure should all remain visible.

## Required movement

- Complete only enough of the second-journey account to establish the delayed
  return and obstruction.
- Let Filby introduce the Permian termination through a recent lecture.
- Establish that deep-time targeting is coarse and the backward range untested.
- Select a compact field kit, including a camera and substantial revolver.
- End on departure rather than extended machine theory.

## Continuity notes

- Preserve the apparent finality of *The Time Machine* epilogue for its
  original witnesses.
- The obstruction is separate from the Permian catastrophe.
- Do not give the Traveller a modern extinction date or mechanism.
- Exact period kit receives a source check immediately before drafting.
