## Chapter purpose

Make the expedition physically credible before its explanation begins. The
notebook should establish procedure, landscape, specimen, and predator while
its shortening entries create a clean appetite for the public account.

## POV and pressure

The narrator observes Filby reading; the notebook supplies the Traveller's
compressed immediate record. Filby's impatience presses against loyalty to his
convalescent friend.

## Required movement

- Show the damaged return and more than a week of convalescence.
- Establish the uncertain arrival and effort of moving the machine into the
  fissure refuge.
- Record a repeatable baseline across land, water, vegetation, and animal life.
- Collect the complete ganoid fish deliberately during calm conditions.
- Mark the large predator by its left-shoulder scar and let it ignore him.
- Shorten later entries before the crisis becomes a coherent narrative.
- End with the Traveller interrupting and demanding a Royal Society venue.

## Continuity notes

- The notebook cannot duplicate Chapters III-V.
- Its voice becomes compressed and fragmentary through condition, not sudden
  modern telegraphese.
- Whether Filby's reading was authorised should cause mild friction only.
- The fish is not a trophy from the predator encounter.
