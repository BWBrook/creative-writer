## Chapter purpose

Move from public framing into an immersive expedition account and demonstrate
how repeated measurement begins to convert unrelated irregularities into a
directional problem.

## POV and pressure

The anonymous narrator briefly establishes the meeting, then the Traveller's
reported first person takes over. He wants to carry a sceptical audience with
him without claiming more than the first interval can support.

## Required movement

- Establish a serious special meeting rather than a theatrical demonstration.
- Bridge past the notebook material with minimal repetition.
- Advance about thirteen months by machine calibration.
- Repeat the same landmarks, measurements, and photographic viewpoints.
- Introduce persistent haze, abnormal heat, river recession, and changed lake
  margins as individually plausible but collectively troubling.
- Show the first material cost of rematerialisation and field repetition.

## Continuity notes

- The hall should recede once the account becomes immersive.
- No modern date, atmospheric chemistry, or global mechanism enters the voice.
- The first interval raises pressure without becoming catastrophe.
- Preserve enough ordinary continuity that change can be measured.
