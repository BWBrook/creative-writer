## Chapter purpose

Convert the adventure back into contested evidence. The examination should
strengthen the case for the journey while narrowing the claims that one site
and damaged sample chain can support.

## POV and pressure

The anonymous narrator regains control. The Traveller faces distinct
disciplinary pressures and must decide which claims to defend, qualify, or
withdraw without becoming either triumphant or meek.

## Required movement

- Dispose of the gadfly's broad disbelief through prior witnesses and material
  evidence rather than a public machine demonstration.
- Let the Geologist test date, stratigraphy, temporal resolution, and the leap
  from Richmond to planetary termination.
- Let the Chemist expose contamination, sealing, and causal limits in the
  surviving samples.
- Let the Comparative Anatomist classify the fish cautiously and challenge the
  certainty of the recurring predator.
- Let the Astronomer ask why the machine follows Earth's moving surface.
- End with the journey largely credited and the global volcanic explanation
  still contested.

## Continuity notes

- Questioners remain occupational identities, not a parade of historical
  celebrities.
- Each major question must add evidence or change confidence.
- No single exhibit proves time travel or the extinction mechanism.
- The Traveller reports terrestrial anchoring as behaviour, not solved theory.
