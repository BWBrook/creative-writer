## Chapter purpose

Close the expedition privately, preserve divided judgement, and leave a small
physical sign that the Traveller's appetite for temporal inquiry has survived.

## POV and pressure

The anonymous narrator observes Filby and the Traveller after public
performance has ended. Filby wants a candid answer about what the journey has
changed; the Traveller resists a simple confession.

## Required movement

- Return to cigars and smoking bishop in the Richmond study.
- Let Filby press the distinction between humiliation, uncertainty, and
  renewed curiosity.
- Preserve the Geologist's strongest objection rather than defeating it in
  retrospect.
- Show altered calculations, settings, or another restrained preparation.
- End before a destination or fresh departure is announced.

## Continuity notes

- The coda must remain oblique.
- Do not resolve the temporal obstruction.
- Do not advertise the next anthology story.
- The final note joins humility and appetite rather than choosing one.
