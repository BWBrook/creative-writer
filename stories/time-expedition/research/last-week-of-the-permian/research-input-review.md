# Verdict

The Pro dossier is a strong external research input. It satisfies the requested
structure, respects the fixed five-act design, keeps modern and late-Victorian
knowledge distinct, and supplies enough evidence to support the next story
architecture pass. No wholesale second research exercise is justified.

It should not be treated as canonical without the corrections below. The
central scientific and historical sources sampled in this review are real and
relevant, but several confidence labels combine direct evidence with useful
fictional construction. The eventual canonical dossier must keep those two
judgements separate.

# Material Corrections

## 1. Two-to-five-year collapse is a story mechanism

The proposed sequence sampled over two to five real years is dramatically
useful, but the cited literature does not establish a comparable terminal
collapse at ancient Richmond over that interval. High-resolution marine work
places the main extinction interval on a scale of tens of thousands of years,
while terrestrial records are regionally asynchronous. A South China study
resolves deterioration and terrestrial disruption on centennial to millennial
scales, not a few years.

The two-to-five-year sequence must therefore be marked **Speculative** as a
local episode selected by temporal sampling. It remains admissible because the
Traveller can cross much longer intervals before remaining continuously
present for the final crisis. The story should not imply that this timescale is
a modern scientific reconstruction.

Supporting checks:

- [Burgess, Bowring and Shen 2014](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1317692111)
- [Wu et al. 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10830061/)
- [Chu et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-37717-0)

## 2. Richmond analogues need two confidence axes

The British Geological Survey directly supports a rocky Permian desert and an
absence of preserved Permian deposits in the London and Thames Valley region.
It does not preserve Richmond's terminal-Permian river, lake, cave, fauna, or
ecological sequence. Those elements are responsible extrapolations from wider
basin-margin and low-latitude analogues.

When the report is distilled, each major choice should carry both:

- an **evidence status** for what the sources establish; and
- a **story-choice status** for whether the extrapolation is coherent and
  approved for this manuscript.

Under that distinction:

- the rocky London-Brabant high is **Supported evidence**;
- the river-playa mosaic is a **Supported story choice** based on regional
  analogy;
- the fissure shelter and flood-assisted roof fall are **Plausible story
  choices**, not reconstructed local geology;
- the ganoid fish is a **Supported period-facing analogue**, not a documented
  Richmond species; and
- the theriodont-grade predator remains **Speculative evidence** and a
  **Provisional story choice**.

Supporting checks:

- [BGS, London and the Thames Valley](https://webapps.bgs.ac.uk/memoirs/docs/B01360.html)
- [JNCC, Fossil Fishes of Great Britain](https://data.jncc.gov.uk/data/f722dcd3-4cf1-46d8-9624-56abff537c8d/gcr-v16-fossil-fishes-of-great-britain-c10.pdf)
- [Bernardi et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.earscirev.2017.10.002)

## 3. Palaeolatitude is verified but should remain period-oblique

The dossier's broad low-northern-latitude placement is sound. A direct
Paleolatitude.org 3.0 API check for modern Richmond coordinates at 251.9 Ma,
using the `vaes` model, returned a mean of 17.34 degrees north with bounds of
15.46 to 19.30 degrees north:

```text
https://paleolatitude.org/api/paleolatitude/51.4613/-0.3037/251.9/251.8/252.0/vaes
```

This supports the modern working description "near 17 degrees north under one
current model". In the manuscript, "within or near the northern tropic" is the
safer expression. The Traveller may estimate latitude astronomically, but no
character should use a modern plate reconstruction or present the result as
exact.

Sources:

- [Paleolatitude.org 3.0](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0346817)
- [Paleolatitude API manual](https://paleolatitude.org/dist/assets/resources/Paleolatitude_API_Manual.pdf)

## 4. Use a special Royal Society meeting

The report recommends a "specially extended ordinary meeting", but direct
period evidence supplies a cleaner precedent. On 31 March 1898 the Royal
Society held a special Thursday meeting at 4.30 p.m. for preliminary statements
about the recent total solar eclipse. A separate Royal Society meeting on 24
February 1898 supported an extended scientific discussion with multiple
speakers.

The story should therefore use a **special meeting of the Royal Society at
Burlington House**, arranged through a Fellow and chaired by the President or a
Vice-President. This naturally accommodates the Traveller's sustained account,
exhibits, and examination without confusing the event with a Royal Institution
Friday Evening Discourse.

Sources:

- [Nature notice of the special eclipse meeting, 1898](https://doi.org/10.1038/057488a0)
- [Nature account of the Royal Society Antarctic meeting, 1898](https://doi.org/10.1038/057413a0)
- [Royal Society conversazione programme, 1896](https://catalogues.royalsociety.org/CalmView/Record.aspx?id=PC%2F3%2F2%2F14&src=CalmView.Catalog)

## 5. Reject the supplied map image

The supplied JPEG, subsequently deleted from the project, is not referenced by
the report and has no recorded creator, date, licence, or source. Visual
comparison identifies it as a map of Pangaea about 200 Ma, associated with
National Geographic educational material.
That is roughly 52 million years later than the story setting and cannot serve
as a Permian reconstruction.

The image was rejected as evidence and should not be published or used in story
planning. A later map should be generated from a cited 251.9 Ma reconstruction
or omitted.

Reference:

- [National Geographic, Pangaea 200 million years ago](https://education.nationalgeographic.org/resource/earths-tectonic-plates/)

# Verified Foundation

The following parts of the report are suitable foundations for canonical
distillation:

- ancient Richmond as an exposed, arid London-Brabant high in the northern
  tropics, with local detail supplied explicitly by analogy;
- distant volcanic forcing expressed through heat, hydrological stress,
  vegetation failure, faunal displacement, and a final extreme runoff event;
- observation and repeated measurements taking precedence over a catalogue of
  speculative kill mechanisms;
- a small ganoid fish in strong spirit as the preferred complete specimen;
- an unnamed theriodont-like predator, kept taxonomically provisional and
  behaviourally ordinary;
- a shallow fissure or rock shelter rather than a mature limestone cavern;
- a compact Victorian kit using repeated controls, labelled samples, simple
  reagents, a barometer, thermometers, survey tools, notebooks, and optional
  photography;
- the modern-to-period terminology boundary, including the absence of Pangaea,
  continental drift, radiometric dating, ecosystem jargon, and the Siberian
  Traps causal model from the characters' knowledge;
- Wells's own observed sequence of thing, comparison, name, inference, and
  qualification;
- canonical machine constraints derived from *The Time Machine*, including
  fixed locality, physical movement while stopped, fatigue, removable levers,
  and the danger of rematerialising into matter; and
- a special Royal Society meeting producing strong but divided acceptance.

Local checks against Wells's attached texts confirmed the quoted scientific
vocabulary and the machine constraints sampled in the report.

# Distillation Boundary

The external report should remain unchanged as provenance. The next pass may
derive a shorter canonical dossier from it and this review, but should not yet:

- draft manuscript prose;
- decompose final chapters or scene cards;
- resolve all ten creative decisions in the external report;
- add research tooling or schemas; or
- undertake another broad literature search.

Only a decisive claim that fails during canonical distillation should trigger
additional research. The highest-value next action is to create the compact
dual-layer canonical dossier, explicitly separating evidence from approved
fictional construction, and return it for project review before detailed scene
architecture.
