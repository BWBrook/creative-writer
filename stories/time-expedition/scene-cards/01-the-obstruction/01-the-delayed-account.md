# The Delayed Account

## Scene purpose

Restore the source-canon social frame and explain why the Traveller returned
later than his witnesses expected after the second departure.

## POV

The anonymous narrator observes the Traveller and Filby in the Richmond study.
He can report manner, interruption, and remembered testimony without entering
the Traveller's private thoughts.

## Pressure

Filby wants a concrete account of the obstruction. The Traveller possesses
observations and failed calculations rather than a satisfying mechanism.

## Turn

The delayed return becomes credible as an effect of the machine's earliest
experiments while remaining unexplained.

## Exit state

The old account is complete enough for the conversation to turn from recovery
to what the Traveller might attempt next.

## Continuity hooks

- Preserve the original epilogue's reason to regard the Traveller as lost.
- Keep the temporal obstruction separate from the Permian catastrophe.
- Do not resolve terrestrial anchoring or temporal mechanics here.
