# Filby's Provocation

## Scene purpose

Join a period scientific uncertainty to the Traveller's mixed motives for his
first deep journey into the past.

## POV

The anonymous narrator follows the study conversation, with attention to
Filby's practical bluntness and the Traveller's intellectual vanity.

## Pressure

Filby describes a lecture on the abrupt disappearance of Permian forms. The
Traveller must admit that his machine has never tested such a backward range
and cannot target a precise geological day.

## Turn

An academic problem becomes a possible expedition.

## Exit state

The Traveller has accepted a broad late-Permian target for reasons that include
science, rivalry, and adventure.

## Continuity hooks

- Use period geological uncertainty rather than modern extinction consensus.
- Filby provokes; he does not become the narrator or expedition companion.
- Preserve coarse targeting and the retrospective meaning of "last week."
