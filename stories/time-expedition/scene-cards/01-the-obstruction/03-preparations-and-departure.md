# Preparations and Departure

## Scene purpose

Define the expedition's material limits and end Act I on irreversible action.

## POV

The narrator sees selected preparations and hears the Traveller justify them.
The inventory should emerge through choice and capacity rather than read as a
complete equipment list.

## Pressure

The machine can carry only a compact kit. Every instrument, provision,
container, camera component, and cartridge displaces something else.

## Turn

The Traveller applies lessons from his first journey, including his regret at
going unarmed, while accepting that equipment cannot remove deep-time risk.

## Exit state

The machine departs backwards with the expedition committed and no guarantee
of accurate arrival.

## Continuity hooks

- Source-check exact camera, firearm, instruments, and preservatives before
  drafting.
- The revolver is carried; it will delay danger rather than solve it.
- End before a prolonged account of the machine's transit.
