# Return and Convalescence

## Scene purpose

Reveal the expedition's cost before explaining its events and create the delay
that makes the notebook necessary.

## POV

The anonymous narrator observes the machine's return, the Traveller's physical
condition, and Filby's increasingly impatient concern over more than a week.

## Pressure

The Traveller cannot sustain an account. Filby wants facts and must decide how
far friendship permits him to inspect the recovered notebook.

## Turn

The immediate question changes from whether the Traveller survived to what
happened during the journey.

## Exit state

The Traveller is recovering upstairs, and Filby has brought the notebook into
the downstairs study.

## Continuity hooks

- Injuries should match the later flood, debris, exertion, and close escape.
- Do not disclose the climax through conspicuously labelled damage.
- Keep Filby's impatience humane rather than acquisitive.
