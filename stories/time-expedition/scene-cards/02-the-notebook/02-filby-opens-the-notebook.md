# Filby Opens the Note-book

## Scene purpose

Establish the notebook as a distinct evidentiary voice and clarify the chain by
which the reader receives it.

## POV

The narrator watches Filby select and read entries. Brief reactions may frame
the record without interrupting every observation.

## Pressure

Filby is unsure whether waiting protects his friend or merely protects the
mystery. The record itself begins orderly enough to reward his decision.

## Turn

The social frame yields to the Traveller's immediate procedural record.

## Exit state

The notebook has begun the backward descent and established the uncertainty of
arrival.

## Continuity hooks

- Decide during drafting whether permission was implied, not explicit.
- The breach should create mild friction when the Traveller returns.
- Notebook syntax may compress gradually; avoid abrupt modern shorthand.
