# Arrival and Refuge

## Scene purpose

Place the machine in ancient Richmond, establish the mixed landscape, and turn
the fissure shelter into a credible field station with future liabilities.

## POV

The notebook records the Traveller's revival, first survey, and physical work
with practical immediacy.

## Pressure

He awakens exposed, uncertain of date and conditions, and must secure the heavy
machine before observation can begin.

## Turn

After dragging the machine into the shallow fissure, he gains shelter, storage,
and a fixed point from which successive landscapes can be compared.

## Exit state

The refuge appears stable and useful; the machine is protected but spatially
committed to the same rock cavity at later stations.

## Continuity hooks

- The refuge is a shallow fissure or rock shelter, not mature karst.
- Ancient Richmond includes ridge, alluvial plain, seasonal river, and terminal
  saline lake as bounded story choices.
- Establish reference marks that later surveys can repeat.
