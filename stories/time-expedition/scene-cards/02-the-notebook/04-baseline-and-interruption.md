# Baseline and Interruption

## Scene purpose

Complete the calm scientific baseline, introduce the specimen and predator,
then let the record fail before it can narrate the crisis.

## POV

Filby's reading carries the Traveller's notebook entries. The narrator observes
the shortening record and the restored Traveller's eventual entrance.

## Pressure

The Traveller must classify an unfamiliar landscape with period concepts. The
later entries show fatigue and isolated irregularities that refuse a single
explanation.

## Turn

The complete fish and scarred predator give the vanished world physical form;
the notebook then becomes too fragmentary to answer the questions it raises.

## Exit state

The Traveller interrupts, objects just enough to Filby's reading to preserve
social friction, and asks him to arrange a Royal Society address.

## Continuity hooks

- Collect the fish deliberately from the lake during calm conditions.
- The predator ignores the Traveller and bears a left-shoulder scar.
- Preserve the later crisis for the immersive lecture account.
- The Traveller chooses public examination rather than private completion.
