# The Special Meeting

## Scene purpose

Establish the authority, social temperature, and evidentiary terms of the Royal
Society occasion before the ancient account becomes immersive.

## POV

The anonymous narrator observes Burlington House, the occupational mixture of
the audience, Filby's role, the exhibits, and the Traveller's manner.

## Pressure

The meeting must take an extraordinary witness seriously without granting him
automatic credibility or turning the machine into a public spectacle.

## Turn

Prior testimony and visible exhibits earn the Traveller an attentive hour, not
an advance verdict.

## Exit state

The Traveller has bridged beyond the notebook's complete entries, and the hall
can recede behind his first-person account.

## Continuity hooks

- Use a special Thursday-afternoon meeting at Burlington House.
- The full machine need not be present; decide only if a scene requires it.
- Keep the opening brief enough to preserve expedition momentum.
