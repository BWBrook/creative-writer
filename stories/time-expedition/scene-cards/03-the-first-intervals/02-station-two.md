# Station II

## Scene purpose

Show the first controlled comparison and establish that individually ordinary
changes become troubling when they recur across the same measured ground.

## POV

The Traveller gives a reported first-person field account shaped by landmarks,
instruments, sample points, and his own provisional expectations.

## Pressure

He rematerialises about thirteen months beyond baseline without knowing whether
the refuge remains open or whether the differences are seasonal, local, or
directional.

## Turn

Persistent haze, greater heat, river recession, and altered lake margins align
without yet compelling a single cause.

## Exit state

The Traveller has enough discrepancy to repeat the experiment and too little
evidence to make a broad claim.

## Continuity hooks

- Reuse the baseline reference marks and photographic viewpoints.
- Keep modern climate chemistry outside the Traveller's language.
- Preserve ordinary surviving life so the next decline has contrast.
