# The Cost of Repetition

## Scene purpose

Make temporal sampling materially useful and materially expensive before the
story asks the Traveller to depend upon it again.

## POV

The Traveller reports the return to the fissure, instrument comparison,
sampling, photography, and physical effects of another deep-time traversal.

## Pressure

Each additional station improves the sequence while exposing him to uncertain
rematerialisation, fatigue, and the possibility that the refuge will no longer
contain clear space.

## Turn

A distant sighting of the shoulder-scarred predator suggests continuity across
the interval, but the animal now watches rather than disregards him.

## Exit state

The Traveller prepares a second forward advance with reduced confidence in the
refuge and increased confidence that change has direction.

## Continuity hooks

- Keep the predator distant and cautious.
- Early photographic material can survive; no image proves identity.
- Do not use machinery failure as artificial reluctance.
