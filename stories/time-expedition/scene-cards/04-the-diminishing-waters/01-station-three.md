# Station III

## Scene purpose

Add independent biological evidence to the physical sequence and eliminate
ordinary seasonal change as a sufficient explanation.

## POV

The Traveller repeats his routes about twenty-three months after baseline,
moving from water to vegetation and animal observations.

## Pressure

Aquatic distress, failing plants, prey movement, and reduced abundance demand
classification while heat, thirst, and fatigue reduce his own reliability.

## Turn

Different living groups show related stress across the same landscape. The
case becomes ecological and directional rather than merely meteorological.

## Exit state

The Traveller rejects a narrow local season as adequate and begins considering
a disturbance acting beyond the visible horizon.

## Continuity hooks

- Let concrete organisms and measured water changes lead each inference.
- Use period comparison and broad anatomical grades, not modern clades.
- Avoid stacking every proposed end-Permian kill mechanism.
