# The Final Advance

## Scene purpose

Place the Traveller at the last occupied station with a deliberate observation
plan, protected core evidence, and a retreat margin he knows is diminishing.

## POV

The Traveller inventories the changed refuge and landscape about thirty months
beyond baseline, then reasons through whether to remain.

## Pressure

Early seepage or stone movement warns that the fissure is less secure. A short
stay would preserve safety; three continuous days might reveal the process
that temporal snapshots have only outlined.

## Turn

He fixes the proof satchel to the machine and chooses continuous observation.

## Exit state

The machine is accessible, selected evidence is secured, and the Traveller is
committed to the personal final days that give the story its title.

## Continuity hooks

- The proof satchel contains the fish; baseline and Station III air tubes and
  water bottles; one Station III deposit packet; and an early exposed film
  roll.
- Notebook and instrument register remain on his person.
- The refuge warning is real but not yet an obvious death trap.
