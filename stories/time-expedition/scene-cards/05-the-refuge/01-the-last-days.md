# The Last Days

## Scene purpose

Convert the sampled deterioration into a continuous witnessed sequence and
prepare the hydrological and animal pressures that converge at the refuge.

## POV

The Traveller observes through exhaustion, returning repeatedly to fixed
marks, the river, the plain, and the shelter entrance.

## Pressure

Heat and water scarcity give way to violent runoff. He wants final samples and
photographs while recognising that the river's new course threatens his only
route to the machine.

## Turn

Floodwater crosses previously dry ground, displaced animals seek the ridge,
and undercutting begins to move debris around the fissure.

## Exit state

The environmental hypothesis has become immediate physical fact; retreat is
still possible only if he abandons the wider archive.

## Continuity hooks

- Make the runoff physically follow the dry, degraded catchment.
- No earthquake or local eruption supplies the crisis.
- Final photographs and most loose samples remain outside the proof satchel.
