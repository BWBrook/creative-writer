# The Breach

## Scene purpose

Bring the scarred predator into the refuge through catastrophe-driven
desperation and demonstrate the revolver's real but limited value.

## POV

The Traveller reports sightlines, distances, shots, animal reaction, and stone
movement with immediate physical precision.

## Pressure

The predator occupies the route between him and the machine as runoff and roof
movement reduce both space and time. Firing towards the mechanism risks his
only escape.

## Turn

The report and a grazing hit turn one rush, but the animal remains alive and
the altered position makes further fire unsafe.

## Exit state

The Traveller has gained moments rather than victory; falling debris now
partly blocks the machine controls.

## Continuity hooks

- No convenient misfire or implausible stopping shot.
- The predator acts from hunger, fear, and refuge-seeking.
- Exact firearm behaviour receives just-in-time source verification.
