# The Machine Recovered

## Scene purpose

Force the final choice between comprehensive evidence and survival, then end
the expedition at the instant its established constraints are satisfied.

## POV

The Traveller's account narrows to debris, leverage, controls, the animal's
position, and the failing roof.

## Pressure

He must clear enough stone to mount and operate the machine. Recovering loose
instruments, plant presses, samples, or camera material would consume the
remaining margin. The revolver is put back in its case beneath his coat so
that both hands can work; it returns as Chapter II establishes.

## Turn

He abandons the archive, reaches the controls, and begins temporal motion as
the refuge loses structural continuity.

## Exit state

The Traveller escapes with the secured exhibits and notebook, including its
instrument readings, while the final evidence and exact fate of the predator
remain behind.

## Continuity hooks

- The machine operates once physically accessible.
- Preserve the complete fish and bounded comparison samples.
- End the ancient account without modern authorial confirmation of cause.
- Before the laboratory was built, a brief homeward stop lets him physically
  restore the forty-yard displacement caused by moving into the cleft. He
  binds his hands after that final hauling. This completes the existing return
  in Chapter II without giving the machine lateral motion.
