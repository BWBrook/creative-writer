# Proof Before Claim

## Scene purpose

Return the reader fully to Burlington House, answer broad disbelief quickly,
and establish the bounded evidence that deserves expert attention.

## POV

The anonymous narrator observes the audience, the chair, the gadfly, Filby,
the Traveller, and the handling of the surviving exhibits.

## Pressure

A minor sceptic attacks the premise as performance. The chair must decide
whether prior witnesses, the notebook, specimen, samples, and photographs
warrant continued examination.

## Turn

Filby and the narrator corroborate earlier demonstrations and departures. The
material chain defeats dismissal without establishing the Traveller's full
interpretation.

## Exit state

The gadfly has lost control of the room, and disciplined specialists can begin
testing claims rather than debating whether to listen.

## Continuity hooks

- No public journey or theatrical machine demonstration.
- The exhibits are impressive as a set and limited individually.
- Keep the gadfly brief so genuine scepticism belongs to the experts.
