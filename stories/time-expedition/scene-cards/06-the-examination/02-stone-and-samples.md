# Stone and Samples

## Scene purpose

Let geology and chemistry test the Traveller's strongest causal argument and
force explicit distinctions among date, sequence, locality, and cause.

## POV

The narrator reports role-shaped questions and answers, including changes in
the room's judgement.

## Pressure

The Geologist challenges the machine-derived interval, absent Richmond strata,
and leap from local decline to Permian termination. The Chemist challenges
seals, contamination, small sample size, and volcanic interpretation.

## Turn

The Traveller defends the ordered local sequence while conceding that neither
the absolute date nor a planetary volcanic cause can be established from the
surviving material.

## Exit state

The journey's empirical seriousness increases as its broadest explanation
becomes more qualified.

## Continuity hooks

- The Geologist is the principal intellectual antagonist, not a fool.
- The Chemist may find suggestive differences without naming modern proxies.
- Every concession should preserve a real observed claim.
