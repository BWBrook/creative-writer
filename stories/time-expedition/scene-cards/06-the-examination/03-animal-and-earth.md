# Animal and Earth

## Scene purpose

Use zoology to validate a vanished fauna and astronomy to expose the machine's
deepest unresolved physical assumption.

## POV

The narrator follows the Comparative Anatomist's inspection and the
Astronomer's conceptual question, allowing their syntax and evidentiary habits
to differ.

## Pressure

The Anatomist can compare the fish to known ganoid forms but cannot certify its
exact age or the predator's identity. The Astronomer asks why rematerialisation
does not leave the machine in empty space as Earth moves.

## Turn

The Traveller can report consistent terrestrial anchoring and offer analogy,
but he cannot supply a mechanism.

## Exit state

The specimen strengthens the case while the anchoring question reveals that
successful use of the machine exceeds its inventor's theory.

## Continuity hooks

- Use period anatomical comparisons and nomenclature.
- The predator remains unnamed and uncollected.
- Terrestrial anchoring follows observed behaviour; avoid modern spacetime
  exposition.
