# A Divided Finding

## Scene purpose

Close the public account with substantial provisional acceptance and a clean
record of what remains disputed.

## POV

The anonymous narrator reads the room through the chair's summary, final
questions, and the contrast between serious attention and withheld assent.

## Pressure

The Traveller must choose whether to overstate his success before an audience
now willing to credit him or preserve the distinction between witness and
theory.

## Turn

He states the hierarchy of his claims: direct observations, reliable local
sequence, probable wider disturbance, and speculative distant cause.

## Exit state

The expedition is credited, the Permian interpretation remains divided, and
the private emotional consequence is reserved for the epilogue.

## Continuity hooks

- Do not give the Traveller an easy victory over the Geologist.
- Keep applause or public sensation restrained.
- Preserve contamination, locality, dating, and anchoring as genuine limits.
