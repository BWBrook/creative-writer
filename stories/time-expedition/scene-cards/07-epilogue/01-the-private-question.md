# The Private Question

## Scene purpose

Remove the Traveller from public performance and let Filby test what the
expedition has changed in him.

## POV

The anonymous narrator observes the Richmond study, cigars, smoking bishop,
and the less guarded exchange between old associates.

## Pressure

Filby wants to know whether incomplete proof has cured the Traveller of his
appetite for temporal danger. The Traveller resists both confession and
boastfulness.

## Turn

He admits that local testimony cannot settle a planetary event, yet treats
that limit as a reason for better inquiry rather than retreat.

## Exit state

Humility and renewed curiosity coexist, and the temporal obstruction remains
the most personal unresolved fact.

## Continuity hooks

- Preserve the Geologist's strongest objection.
- Avoid a modern statement of scientific method.
- Keep Filby active and sceptically affectionate.
