# Altered Settings

## Scene purpose

End on a restrained physical indication of renewed purpose while preserving
the recovered anthology's open future.

## POV

The anonymous narrator notices what Filby notices: revised figures, a changed
scale, moved tools, or another small preparation near the machine.

## Pressure

Any explicit destination or declaration would turn implication into serial
advertisement and weaken the story's closure.

## Turn

The signs establish preparation without revealing whether the Traveller is
testing the obstruction, refining terrestrial anchoring, or choosing a new
expedition.

## Exit state

The manuscript closes with the Traveller chastened, active, and unreadable in
his final intention.

## Continuity hooks

- Select one physical sign during drafting; do not accumulate several.
- Name no destination.
- Echo the restraint of *The Time Machine* epilogue without copying its image
  or cadence.
