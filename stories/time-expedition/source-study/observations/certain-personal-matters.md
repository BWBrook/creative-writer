# Observation Dossier: Certain Personal Matters

## Development state

Status: **development**. This Tier 3 witness supplies bounded nonfiction
comparisons within essay voice, humour, conversation, and microscopic
observation. It has no fictional continuity authority and does not prescribe
narrative voice directly. Front matter and the contents list are editorial
structures, not evidence for the claims below.

## Observations

### Observation 1

#### Passage
- `certain-personal-matters:of-conversation:58:1e55fc6db37a`

#### Observation
The first-person speaker turns an admitted social failure into a self-portrait,
first reporting lost friends and then redescribing his silence as refinement
and consideration.

#### Inference
The essay voice can regulate narrator distance by exposing conduct while
withholding a stable verdict on the speaker; self-account and self-mockery
remain superimposed.

#### Jurisdiction
- Dimension: `narrator-distance`
- Role: `essay voice`

#### Comparison
- related: `certain-personal-matters:the-writing-of-essays:255:60e7e43f4b48` - Another essay persona makes self-description and comic self-exposure operate together.

#### Limitation
Both passages are comic nonfiction personae. They show role-bounded mediation,
not the distance appropriate to a fictional witness or narrator.

#### Candidate claims
- `role-shaped-narrator-distance`

### Observation 3

#### Passage
- `certain-personal-matters:through-a-microscope:347:7b18b6dc9623`

#### Observation
The description selects visible motion and shape first: amoeboid extension and
withdrawal, the diatom's case-like outline, and its unexplained glide. It then
makes those details legible through homely and large-scale analogies.

#### Inference
Microscopic description is selective rather than exhaustive: a few observable
features support vivid comparison while the observer's ignorance remains
explicit.

#### Jurisdiction
- Dimension: `diction-description`
- Role: `microscopic observation`

#### Comparison
- variation: `certain-personal-matters:through-a-microscope:350:7c9d58d283ac` - The later passage expands from microscopic description into a cosmological observer analogy: the microscopist may stand to a further observer as the organisms stand to him.

#### Limitation
That expansion changes both scale and mechanism, bounding the focal claim to
concrete local observation and comparison rather than the essay's later
cosmological analogy.

#### Candidate claims
- `selective-observed-description`

### Observation 4

#### Passage
- `certain-personal-matters:the-pleasure-of-quarrelling:359:58b49bd92afb`

#### Observation
A hypothetical victim's trivial reference to mustard is recast as a personal
slight through staged quotation. The passage presents a quarreller's gambit and
predicts the victim's apologetic response; it does not narrate a completed
interlocutor exchange.

#### Inference
Humour arises from the designed mismatch between ordinary conversation and the
quarreller's contrived interpretation. The staged speech demonstrates the
proposed tactic without supplying an observed reply from the victim.

#### Jurisdiction
- Dimension: `humour-irony`
- Role: `humour`

#### Comparison
- variation: `certain-personal-matters:the-pleasure-of-quarrelling:353:5d0691c29fcf` - The earlier passage builds a mock-scientific case from hygienic, physiological, and instinctive claims, an alternate comic mechanism to the focal social gambit.

#### Limitation
Both examples belong to an overtly comic essay that sustains an inverted case
for quarrelling. They do not establish that social friction is humorous outside
this nonfiction role.

#### Candidate claims
- `humour-through-social-friction`
