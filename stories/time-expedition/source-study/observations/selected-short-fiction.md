## Observations

### Observation 1

#### Passage
- `selected-short-fiction/the-plattner-story:the-plattner-story:1:725f01ec6e50`

#### Observation
The opening of *The Plattner Story* weighs several witnesses and an anatomical
fact against ordinary disbelief, acknowledges the narrator's unresolved
suspicion, and then offers to relate the account without further comment.

#### Inference
The story establishes its speaking situation as an overtly mediated assessment
of testimony rather than an unqualified report.

#### Jurisdiction
- Dimension: `frame-architecture`
- Role: `testimony`

#### Comparison
- variation: `selected-short-fiction/the-crystal-egg:the-crystal-egg:24:99161d95fcc5` - *The Crystal Egg* identifies Wace as the source of the narrative particulars, a related but less openly adversarial mediation of evidence.

#### Limitation
The narrator's profession of balance is itself part of the frame and does not
demonstrate neutrality or validate Plattner's account.

#### Candidate claims
- `mediated-testimony-frame`

### Observation 2

#### Passage
- `selected-short-fiction/the-sea-raiders:ii:19:013c203950dd`

#### Observation
In *The Sea Raiders*, after the men disturb the water, they stop in order to
see more clearly; when the surface settles, the sea bottom appears to be set
with eyes.

#### Inference
A pause for observation converts ambiguous movement into apprehended
biological danger before the creatures attack the boat.

#### Jurisdiction
- Dimension: `suspense-scene-pressure`
- Role: `biological danger`

#### Comparison
- related: `selected-short-fiction/the-sea-raiders:ii:21:4c409fc107a3` - Previously tested as possible resistance, the adjacent eyes, tentacles, parted weeds, and enlarging forms instead extend the same observational disclosure into the creatures' ascent.

#### Limitation
The passages are adjacent stages of one encounter, so they show scene
construction within *The Sea Raiders*, not independent replication across
stories.

#### Candidate claims
- `danger-through-observation`

### Observation 3

#### Passage
- `selected-short-fiction/the-sea-raiders:iii:41:670a0256413b`

#### Observation
*The Sea Raiders* closes a dated sequence of carcasses and disappearances by
calling one attack the last fact available, then immediately qualifies whether
it will prove to be the creatures' final appearance.

#### Inference
The ending closes the evidentiary record while leaving the biological threat
unresolved.

#### Jurisdiction
- Dimension: `transitions-ending-logic`
- Role: `short-form architecture`

#### Comparison
- related: `selected-short-fiction/the-crystal-egg:the-crystal-egg:51:025f5af2df0e` - Previously tested as resistance, *The Crystal Egg* instead reinforces the bounded architecture by ending the object's traceable custody while leaving its implications unresolved.

#### Limitation
The two independent witnesses end different kinds of record: reported attacks
in *The Sea Raiders* and an object's traceable custody in *The Crystal Egg*.
The comparison therefore supports only a bounded architectural relation.

#### Candidate claims
- `short-form-evidentiary-closure`

### Observation 4

#### Passage
- `selected-short-fiction/the-crystal-egg:the-crystal-egg:38:10e7d6a3eb44`

#### Observation
In *The Crystal Egg*, Cave watches and reports, Wace writes in the dark, and
later questions are used to clarify difficult points after the crystal fades.

#### Inference
The story distributes perception, recording, and follow-up questioning between
named participants, making the extraordinary account explicitly mediated
testimony.

#### Jurisdiction
- Dimension: `frame-architecture`
- Role: `testimony`

#### Comparison
- variation: `selected-short-fiction/the-plattner-story:the-plattner-story:17:361fdbf88b7c` - *The Plattner Story* uses a different chain: recollection is written after the interview and subsequently reviewed by Plattner.

#### Limitation
These are represented procedures inside the stories; their detail supports a
claim about frame architecture, not the truth of the reported phenomena.

#### Candidate claims
- `mediated-testimony-frame`
