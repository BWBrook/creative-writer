# Observation Dossier: Text Book of Biology, Part 1

## Development state

Status: **development**. This Tier 3 witness supplies bounded nonfiction
comparisons within biological terminology, instructional exposition, and
comparison and classification. It has no fictional continuity authority, does
not prescribe narrative voice directly, and carries no authority for
post-1890s scientific knowledge. The signed introduction is editorial matter
by G. B. Howes and is excluded from Wells voice evidence; lists and tables are
treated only within their instructional function.

## Observations

### Observation 1

#### Passage
- `text-book-of-biology-part-1:9-classificatory-points:186:a0fe8a3fc993`

#### Observation
Technical terms are introduced through a sequence of contrasts: metazoon
against protozoon, bilateral against radial symmetry, and internal asymmetry
against an inferred symmetrical plan, with named organs supplying concrete
instances.

#### Inference
Biological description selects anatomical features that discriminate among
categories and defines terminology through comparison rather than through an
isolated glossary formula.

#### Jurisdiction
- Dimension: `diction-description`
- Role: `biological terminology`

#### Comparison
- variation: `text-book-of-biology-part-1:9-classificatory-points:187:e2e86fbccce4` - The adjacent passage shifts from symmetry to metameric segmentation, defining serial repetition through vertebrae, nerves, an earthworm, a train, and a string of beads.

#### Limitation
The passage supports terminology and descriptive selection, not the present-day
correctness of its historical-developmental inference.

### Observation 4

#### Passage
- `text-book-of-biology-part-1:note-on-making-comparisons:527:75c6e3818b0d`

#### Observation
Parallel entries compare dog-fish, rabbit, and frog vertebrae feature by
feature, retaining uncertainty where rib homology is described as doubtful.

#### Inference
Comparison and classification can be organised around homologous features
while preserving a local qualification instead of forcing every item into an
unqualified equivalence.

#### Jurisdiction
- Dimension: `scientific-exposition`
- Role: `comparison and classification`

exposition. They support comparison structure and qualification only, not
