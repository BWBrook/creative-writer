### Observation 1

#### Passage
- `the-island-of-doctor-moreau:i:2:522a64e3a5eb`

#### Observation
Prendick begins with the already published account of the Lady Vain, marks one point as supposition, corrects the public record, and closes by naming himself as direct evidence.

#### Inference
The retrospective first-person role establishes distance before testimony: shared documentary knowledge is acknowledged first, then narrowed and revised by the narrator's personal authority.

#### Jurisdiction
- Dimension: `narrator-distance`
- Role: `retrospective first-person narration`

### Observation 2

#### Passage
- `the-island-of-doctor-moreau:xxii:742:1a01256f2dfe`

#### Observation
The narrator reports recurring fear from a later temporal position, labels his perception of other people an illusion, asserts that they are human, and then admits that this knowledge does not prevent him from shrinking away.

#### Inference
Retrospective distance here permits explicit self-correction without resolving embodied uncertainty. The narrating self can judge the experiencing self's belief while preserving the belief's continuing force.

#### Jurisdiction
- Dimension: `narrator-distance`
- Role: `retrospective first-person narration`

#### Comparison
- variation: `the-island-of-doctor-moreau:i:2:522a64e3a5eb` - The opening correction confidently replaces a mistaken public account, whereas the late correction coexists with unresolved private dread.

#### Limitation
The passage is an extreme post-island reflection and does not show that every retrospective correction in the witness remains emotionally unresolved.

### Observation 3

#### Passage
- `the-island-of-doctor-moreau:xiv:393:f95f34e93225`

#### Observation
Moreau states his thesis about the plasticity of living forms, moves through physiology, inoculation, transfusion, historical examples, and tissue grafting, and qualifies parts of the sequence with "may," "probably," and "perhaps."

#### Inference
Within scientific argument, exposition advances by linking an extraordinary claim to a graded series of more familiar procedures. Modal qualification remains present even though Moreau speaks with strong personal confidence.

#### Jurisdiction
- Dimension: `scientific-exposition`
- Role: `scientific argument`

#### Comparison
- related: `the-island-of-doctor-moreau:xiv:406:6922e5aaff9f` - Previously tested as resistance, the pain argument remains qualified by possibility language and is retained as related analytical history.

#### Limitation
The passage records Moreau's argument, not an endorsement of its scientific validity, ethics, or historical accuracy; its expository pattern is bounded to this role.

#### Candidate claims
- `qualified-scientific-exposition`

### Observation 4

#### Passage
- `the-island-of-doctor-moreau:xiii:366:c305934608be`

#### Observation
During Prendick's threatened suicide, Moreau delivers one uninterrupted speech organised by "in the first place," repeated "in the next," and "besides." It combines counterfactual tests, an appeal to Montgomery's character, and a final rhetorical question.

#### Inference
This focal passage is not a distribution of speech, silence, and gesture. It is one extended, explicitly structured argument delivered under danger to make the frightened interlocutor reconsider his inference.

#### Jurisdiction
- Dimension: `dialogue-social-friction`
- Role: `dialogue under stress`

#### Comparison
- variation: `the-island-of-doctor-moreau:xiii:363:b0025c09a139` - Prendick's reply in the same confrontation is deliberately "Short and sharp," providing a resistant turn against any uniform stress rhythm.

#### Limitation
The contrast is local to one confrontation and does not justify pooling this role with the Invisible Man's sustained dialogue, social register, or comic friction.

#### Candidate claims
- `dialogue-pressure-varies-by-context`
