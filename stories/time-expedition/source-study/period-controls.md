# Period Controls

Tier 4 material is used only for negative and comparative discrimination. A
shared feature can remain historically and Wells-characteristic without being
distinctive; a local difference from one control cannot establish uniqueness.

## Claim `mediated-testimony-frame`

### Comparison `beetle-mediated-narrators`

**Wells claim under test:** visible testimony, transcription, or evidentiary
assessment mediates extraordinary events while credibility and uncertainty
remain in view.

**Authorising control role:** `multiple narrators` in `the-beetle`.

**Comparison:** The control labels Holt's contribution as a narration, presents
Champnell's conclusion as a case-book extract, and later discloses that Holt's
record was compiled from statements. The broad mediation device is therefore
`shared`.

**Quantitative context:** The control partition has 113,294 words in 3,237
passages, a median sentence length of 11 words, and a measured dialogue
proportion of 0.475. The held-out *Time Machine* and *Star* partitions differ
substantially (median sentence lengths 15 and 22; dialogue proportions 0.907
and 0.076), so those aggregates do not explain the shared documentary device.

**Control passages:** `the-beetle:book-i-the-house-with-the-open-window:54:8440a05cc8de`,
`the-beetle:book-iv-in-pursuit:2249:a031a184a704`, and
`the-beetle:chapter-xlviii:3234:45875e9c723b`.

**Corrected classification:** `wells_characteristic_but_shared`.

**Prohibited inference:** This comparison does not make Marsh a positive style
authority, establish a general Victorian rule, imply stylistic equivalence, or
support an authorship judgement.

## Claim `role-shaped-narrator-distance`

### Comparison `beetle-role-shaped-distance`

**Wells claim under test:** selected narrating roles create different relations
among represented experience, later judgement, and self-presentation.

**Authorising control role:** `multiple narrators` in `the-beetle`.

**Comparison:** Holt separates possible fact from imagination, Atherton recasts
experience as a doubtful sworn statement, and Marjorie corrects earlier
understanding through hindsight. The broad narrator-distance tendency is
`shared`.

**Quantitative context:** First-person pronouns occur at 63.42 per 1,000 words
in the control, compared with 69.99 in held-out *The Time Machine*, 67.11 in
*Moreau*, and 24.85 in *Certain Personal Matters*. These source-wise rates show
different first-person densities but do not measure retrospective distance.

**Control passages:** `the-beetle:chapter-ix:396:ff76f6bcefc4`,
`the-beetle:chapter-xviii:1235:b508bc2e1193`, and
`the-beetle:chapter-xxiii:1752:eec3779ebde5`.

**Corrected classification:** `wells_characteristic_but_shared`.

**Prohibited inference:** The comparison cannot identify narrative role as the
cause of distance, profile Marsh positively, establish period prevalence, or
support style or authorship attribution.

## Claim `qualified-scientific-exposition`

### Comparison `days-qualified-scientific-exposition`

**Wells claim under test:** unfamiliar scientific claims are linked to
observation, analogy, procedure, or classification while retaining local
qualification.

**Authorising control role:** `technical-work control` in `the-days-work`.

**Comparison:** An engineer compares the bridge with the observed flood,
recalculates component limits, and retains possible embankment failure and
unknown scour. Technical explanation with bounded uncertainty is `shared`.

**Quantitative context:** The control records 0.92 epistemic markers and 11.08
modal verbs per 1,000 words. Held-out *The Time Machine*, *Moreau*, and the
biology textbook record epistemic rates of 5.28, 2.41, and 1.51 and modal rates
of 12.09, 7.62, and 8.67. These whole-witness values do not measure whether an
individual explanation is qualified.

**Control passage:** `the-days-work:the-bridge-builders:72:c8e7cc2f7612`.

**Corrected classification:** `wells_characteristic_but_shared`; final status
is qualified because held-out Moreau is more assertive than expected.

**Prohibited inference:** The comparison neither supplies positive Kipling
guidance nor demonstrates a general technical register, stylistic equivalence,
or authorship.

## Claim `short-form-evidentiary-closure`

### Comparison `solomon-journey-closure`

**Wells claim under test:** the sampled frame and short-form records end while
larger implications remain unresolved.

**Authorising control role:** `journey architecture` in
`king-solomons-mines`.

**Comparison:** The control closes its history, compresses the return, confirms
safe arrival, and ends with a practical decision. Principal outcomes are
settled rather than left as an unresolved evidentiary implication, so the
feature is `not_shared` in this target.

**Quantitative context:** The control's median sentence length is 17 words and
dialogue proportion is 0.376. Held-out *The Time Machine* records 15 and 0.907,
while *The Star* records 22 and 0.076. These whole-witness aggregates do not
measure ending logic.

**Control passages:** `king-solomons-mines:xx:1521:b6ceb2d61e7f`,
`king-solomons-mines:xx:1522:fccb8920e6ba`, and
`king-solomons-mines:xx:1525:a22ed968478c`.

**Corrected classification:** `role_specific`; final status is qualified because
the held-out *Time Machine* window omits the terminal frame.

**Prohibited inference:** One non-shared journey ending does not prove Wellsian
distinctiveness, define Haggard positively, generalise across period endings,
or support authorship.
