# Wells Descriptive Profile

## Final validated state

Status: **validated Phase 2B descriptive profile**. This profile reconciles the
frozen development evidence with predeclared held-out Wells assessments,
negative/comparative controls, and distributed independent review. *The Time
Machine* remains the sole continuity and primary fictional voice authority.
Tier 2 and Tier 3 evidence remains bounded to its declared analytical roles,
with Tier 3 serving as nonfiction comparison rather than fictional voice
authority. Tier 4 evidence is used only to test discrimination and supplies no
positive style authority.

## Analytical dimensions

### 1. Frame architecture and reported testimony

#### Finding 1

##### Claim IDs

- `mediated-testimony-frame`

##### Finding

Across the sampled frame-architecture and testimony passages, extraordinary
events are mediated through visible acts of testimony, transcription, or
evidentiary assessment. The mediation leaves both credibility and uncertainty
in view within these passages.

##### Classification and confidence

Classification: `wells_characteristic_but_shared`
Confidence: `moderate`

##### Jurisdiction

Validated, cross-work finding for frame architecture and testimony in
*The Time Machine*, *The Plattner Story*, and *The Crystal Egg*. *The Time
Machine* alone has continuity and primary voice authority; the two selected
short stories supply bounded Tier 2 testimony comparisons.

##### Evidence

- `the-time-machine:ii:101:b859a93fec21` (Tier 1) makes the transfer from a
  witnessed speaking situation to an incomplete written account explicit.
- `selected-short-fiction/the-plattner-story:the-plattner-story:1:725f01ec6e50`
  (Tier 2) opens through an overt assessment of testimony and unresolved
  suspicion.
- `selected-short-fiction/the-crystal-egg:the-crystal-egg:38:10e7d6a3eb44`
  (Tier 2) distributes observation, recording, and follow-up questioning among
  named participants.

##### Variation and counterexamples

The source and stability of mediation vary: faltering memory, retrospective
written recollection, and participant transcription create different chains
of access. A targeted development search across the three eligible witnesses
found no genuine resistant development passage; each searched case still made
testimony, transcription, or evidentiary assessment visible.

##### Quantitative context

The development metrics do not operationalize visible mediation or
evidentiary assessment. In particular, the quoted-span classifier counts most
of *The Time Machine*'s reported account as dialogue (22,375 of 24,773 words),
so that source-level value is not evidence about conversational exchange or
the frame mechanism described here.

##### Control distinction

Held-out frame and testimony passages repeat the mediation pattern, but the
control's explicit narration and compiled statements show that the broad
device is shared with neighbouring prose rather than corpus-distinctive.

##### Unresolved uncertainty

The result remains limited to sampled frame and testimony roles; neither its
frequency across Wells nor its discrimination from wider period practice is
established by one control witness.

### 2. Narrator distance, focalisation, and mediation

#### Finding 1

##### Claim IDs

- `role-shaped-narrator-distance`

##### Finding

Among the sampled role-context passages, reported testimony interrupts
remembered experience from the present telling, retrospective survival
narration establishes documentary distance, and an essay persona layers
self-account with self-mockery.

##### Classification and confidence

Classification: `wells_characteristic_but_shared`
Confidence: `moderate`

##### Jurisdiction

Validated, cross-work comparison of reported first-person account in
*The Time Machine*, retrospective first-person narration in *The Island of
Doctor Moreau*, and essay voice in *Certain Personal Matters*. The Tier 3 essay
passage is a nonfiction comparison and carries no fictional continuity or
voice authority.

##### Evidence

- `the-time-machine:ix:247:7c7656f6a229` (Tier 1) interrupts remembered grief
  from the later telling and then returns to the experienced scene.
- `the-island-of-doctor-moreau:i:2:522a64e3a5eb` (Tier 2) places personal
  testimony against an existing documentary account.
- `certain-personal-matters:of-conversation:58:1e55fc6db37a` (Tier 3) combines
  comic self-description and self-exposure in essay voice.

##### Variation and counterexamples

The targeted development search found substantial variation in how present
judgement relates to represented experience, but no genuine resistant passage
that removed narrator distance. The Moreau closing passage
`the-island-of-doctor-moreau:xxii:742:1a01256f2dfe` retains self-correction
alongside continuing embodied fear and was treated as related variation, not
as a material counterexample.

##### Quantitative context

Witness-level pronoun, sentence, and quotation counts do not identify temporal
position, documentary mediation, self-mockery, or the relation between an
experiencing and narrating self. The metrics therefore provide sampling
provenance only for this interpretive finding and are not narrator-distance
norms.

##### Control distinction

Held-out Wells passages confirm the three declared narrator-distance
relations, while the multi-narrator control shows comparable retrospective
adjustment outside Wells.

##### Unresolved uncertainty

Role, narrator, work, and episode remain aliased. The evidence validates the
selected contrasts but cannot identify role as their cause or establish a
general period distribution.

### 3. Observation, inference, speculation, and self-correction

#### Finding 1

##### Claim IDs

- `qualified-speculative-inference`

##### Finding

In the sampled speculative-method and evidence-to-speculation passages,
movement from observed detail to explanation is marked as provisional,
revisable, or vulnerable to perceptual error.

##### Classification and confidence

Classification: `wells_characteristic_but_shared`
Confidence: `moderate`

##### Jurisdiction

Validated, cross-work finding for speculative method in *The Time
Machine* and evidence-to-speculation movement in *The War of the Worlds*. It
describes transitions within the cited fictional passages, not the scientific
truth of their explanations. *The Time Machine* alone carries continuity
authority.

##### Evidence

- `the-time-machine:iv:147:5a08c0c8cfcb` (Tier 1) retrospectively identifies a
  simple, plausible causal account as mistaken.
- `the-war-of-the-worlds:i:10:dc75deedfa61` (Tier 2) moves from attributed
  observations to a grammatically provisional explanation.
- `the-war-of-the-worlds:ii:27:b70ea6d8ec6a` (Tier 2) records Ogilvy's narrow
  initial attribution before the complete trigger for revision available
  outside the indexed development passage.

##### Variation and counterexamples

`the-time-machine:xi:264:2537c034e240` is a genuine resistant development
case retained as variation: astronomical observations lead to a categorical
tidal-drag explanation without local modal qualification or correction. The
two *War of the Worlds* passages are separate passages from one witness, not
independent replications.

##### Quantitative context

Counts of modal and epistemic terms can locate candidate passages, but they do
not measure the semantic sequence from observation to explanation, whether a
revision occurs, or whether a perception is mistaken. No pooled rate is used;
the finding rests on close reading of two independent witnesses.

##### Control distinction

Held-out Wells passages confirm revisable inference, but the control also
moves from observed circumstances to a viewpoint-limited explanation.

##### Unresolved uncertainty

One control occurrence cannot separate author, genre, focalisation, or period,
and the categorical development variation still limits claims about
frequency.

### 4. Scientific exposition, analogy, qualification, and uncertainty

#### Finding 1

##### Claim IDs

- `qualified-scientific-exposition`

##### Finding

Across the sampled role-context passages, scientific exposition links
unfamiliar claims to observations, analogies, procedures, or classifications
while retaining different local qualifications.

##### Classification and confidence

Classification: `wells_characteristic_but_shared`
Confidence: `moderate`

##### Jurisdiction

Validated, cross-work comparison of speculative method in *The Time
Machine*, scientific argument in *The Island of Doctor Moreau*, and
instructional exposition plus comparison and classification in *Text Book of
Biology, Part 1*. The textbook is Tier 3 nonfiction comparison only and has no
fictional continuity or direct fictional voice authority.

##### Evidence

- `the-time-machine:v:165:29fe69fce8b4` (Tier 1) uses analogy to bound the
  observer's access and resulting general impression rather than to supply a
  procedure.
- `the-island-of-doctor-moreau:xiv:393:f95f34e93225` (Tier 2) links an
  extraordinary claim to observations, analogies, and a graded series of
  procedures while retaining modal qualification.
- `text-book-of-biology-part-1:2-the-theory-of-evolution:472:f51263a926c6`
  (Tier 3) corrects a likely misconception and qualifies a biological
  relation.
- `text-book-of-biology-part-1:note-on-making-comparisons:527:75c6e3818b0d`
  (Tier 3) compares homologous features while retaining local uncertainty.

##### Variation and counterexamples

The devices differ materially by role: analogy bounds access in one passage,
argument assembles procedures in another, and textbook entries correct or
classify. `the-time-machine:xi:264:2537c034e240` is resistant variation because
its tidal-drag account gives a more categorical causal explanation with less
local qualification.

##### Quantitative context

Modal and epistemic frequencies do not identify whether an unfamiliar claim
is linked to observation, analogy, procedure, or classification, and the
textbook's lists, notation, and 503 words of unresolved quote structure limit
sentence and quotation summaries. The metrics are source-local sampling aids,
not expository norms or evidence of fictional voice.

##### Control distinction

Held-out exposition generally retains observation or procedure with local
bounds, although Moreau is more assertive; the technical control shares the
broad combination of explanation and acknowledged uncertainty.

##### Unresolved uncertainty

Expository role, work, and subject remain confounded, and qualification is
uneven within Wells. The evidence does not establish a Wells-specific degree
or frequency of epistemic restraint.

### 5. Dialogue, speech attribution, class, and social friction

#### Finding 1

##### Claim IDs

- `dialogue-pressure-varies-by-context`

##### Finding

Among the sampled role-context passages, village group talk is distributed
across brief voices, a coercive exchange pairs a broken threat with a long
appeasing reply, and argument under danger takes the form of one extended,
ordered speech.

##### Classification and confidence

Classification: `wells_characteristic_but_shared`
Confidence: `moderate`

##### Jurisdiction

Validated, cross-work comparison of social register and sustained
dialogue in *The Invisible Man* with dialogue under stress in *The Island of
Doctor Moreau*. Both are Tier 2 bounded role evidence and carry no continuity
authority.

##### Evidence

- `the-invisible-man:iii:92:8c3779ecfb78` distributes village response across
  social labels, dialect utterances, and narratorial summary.
- `the-invisible-man:ix:342:8ec129f34188` pairs an incomplete threat and
  physical cue with a long, self-correcting appeasing turn.
- `the-island-of-doctor-moreau:xiii:366:c305934608be` presents one extended
  argument ordered by explicit stages during a dangerous confrontation.

##### Variation and counterexamples

`the-invisible-man:xiii:444:6e6a04602732` is resistant variation: a later,
complete threat silences Marvel and transfers response from speech to gesture.
The same relationship therefore does not yield a fixed conversational rhythm,
and the Moreau confrontation also contains a short, sharp resistant turn.

##### Quantitative context

The quoted-span classifier records 20,122 of 38,241 words as dialogue in *The
Invisible Man* and 8,926 of 36,061 in *Moreau*, with 1,273 and 40 words,
respectively, unresolved by the quote classifier. These source-wide
denominators locate dialogue-rich material but do not distinguish turn shape,
social class, coercion, interruption, or scene function and are not dialogue
targets.

##### Control distinction

Held-out Wells exchanges confirm that pressured dialogue has no uniform local
organisation, while the control shows comparable distribution across voices
and reactions in group dialogue.

##### Unresolved uncertainty

Speaker, scene, work, and pressure remain confounded. The evidence validates
variation in the selected contexts, not a causal effect of pressure or a
general dialogue rhythm. The modern *Invisible Man* transcription also keeps
paragraph-level interpretation provisional.

### 6. Syntax, rhythm, punctuation, and paragraph movement

#### Finding 1

##### Claim IDs

- `role-shaped-syntax`

##### Finding

The sampled reported-experiment, sustained-exchange, and essay-address
passages have contrasting local sentence and paragraph movements rather than
one uniform cadence.

##### Classification and confidence

Classification: `wells_characteristic_but_shared`
Confidence: `moderate`

##### Jurisdiction

Validated, cross-work comparison of reported first-person account in
*The Time Machine*, sustained dialogue in *The Invisible Man*, and essay voice
in *Certain Personal Matters*. The essay is Tier 3 nonfiction comparison only;
*The Time Machine* alone has continuity and primary voice authority.

##### Evidence

- `the-time-machine:iii:102:301f3d1a7670` uses coordinated clauses and
  semicolons for procedural reconstruction, then shorter units around shock
  and verification.
- `the-invisible-man:ix:342:8ec129f34188` creates local movement through a
  broken threat, physical interruption, and a long appeasing response.
- `certain-personal-matters:the-writing-of-essays:263:4f9b481f9634` builds
  cumulative imperative and analogical sequences before a short close.

##### Variation and counterexamples

`certain-personal-matters:the-writing-of-essays:261:7635cd06f9a3` replaces the
nearby cumulative essay movement with three short declaratives. Crisis syntax
in `the-time-machine:ix:240:d5a4f7619d0a` and the shorter later threat in *The
Invisible Man* add within-role variation, so none of the sampled roles has a
fixed local rhythm.

##### Quantitative context

Development medians differ at witness scale: sentence and paragraph medians
are 15 and 97.5 words for *The Time Machine*, 9 and 23 for *The Invisible
Man*, and 16 and 134 for *Certain Personal Matters*. These are whole-witness
partition summaries, not measurements of the focal passages; sentence
boundaries are approximations, role is aliased with work, and *The Invisible
Man* paragraphing is transcription-sensitive. The values are not cadence
norms.

##### Control distinction

Held-out Wells passages repeat the declared local rhythm contrasts, while the
professional-dialogue control also alternates compact and extended movements.

##### Unresolved uncertainty

Role remains inseparable from speaker, scene, event, and work. The evidence
does not yield a stable syntactic norm or a Wells-distinctive cadence, and
paragraph transcription remains a material limitation.

### 7. Diction, figurative language, and descriptive selectivity

#### Finding 1

##### Claim IDs

- `selective-observed-description`

##### Finding

Across the sampled role-context passages, concrete details are organized
around a local act of observation, comparison, or classification: a changed
landscape, a microscopic form, or an anatomical category.

##### Classification and confidence

Classification: `wells_characteristic_but_shared`
Confidence: `tentative`

##### Jurisdiction

Validated, cross-work comparison of landscape observation in *The War
of the Worlds*, microscopic observation in *Certain Personal Matters*, and
biological terminology in *Text Book of Biology, Part 1*. The latter two are
Tier 3 nonfiction comparisons and do not provide fictional continuity or
direct fictional voice authority.

##### Evidence

- `the-war-of-the-worlds:v:101:8debc943c595` (Tier 2) selects spatial, color,
  smoke, and fire details to organize observation of the altered common.
- `certain-personal-matters:through-a-microscope:347:7b18b6dc9623` (Tier 3)
  selects visible motion and shape for microscopic comparison while retaining
  observer ignorance.
- `text-book-of-biology-part-1:9-classificatory-points:186:a0fe8a3fc993`
  (Tier 3) selects anatomical features that discriminate among categories.

##### Variation and counterexamples

`certain-personal-matters:through-a-microscope:350:7c9d58d283ac` is resistant
variation because it expands from local microscopic description into a
cosmological observer analogy. The textbook also shifts from symmetry to
serial segmentation in an adjacent passage, changing both selected features
and comparisons.

##### Quantitative context

Word, sentence, paragraph, and lexical-concentration measurements do not
classify a detail as observational, comparative, anatomical, figurative, or
selective. Witness-level values also cannot recover what was omitted from a
description. The quantitative profile therefore supplies provenance and
sampling scale only, with no pooled descriptive norm.

##### Control distinction

Held-out landscape and biological passages retain observation-led selection,
while the microscope passage mixes it with comic speculation; the machinery
control shares functional descriptive organisation.

##### Unresolved uncertainty

Selection is inferred from stated organisation rather than observable
omission, and role, subject, work, humour, and analogy remain confounded.
Distinctive frequency cannot be estimated.

### 8. Suspense, revelation, danger, and scene pressure

#### Finding 1

##### Claim IDs

- `danger-through-observation`

##### Finding

In the sampled biological-danger and catastrophe passages, dangerous
phenomena are disclosed through accumulating observable consequences before
explanatory closure, with different scale and tempo in the two events.

##### Classification and confidence

Classification: `wells_characteristic_but_shared`
Confidence: `tentative`

##### Jurisdiction

Validated, cross-work comparison of biological danger in *The Sea
Raiders* and catastrophe in *The War of the Worlds*. Both are Tier 2 evidence
within their declared roles; neither has continuity authority.

##### Evidence

- `selected-short-fiction/the-sea-raiders:ii:19:013c203950dd` converts
  ambiguous movement into apprehended danger when a pause permits clearer
  observation of the sea bottom.
- `the-war-of-the-worlds:v:98:0fa76c4fe402` discloses catastrophic danger
  through a falling person and igniting vegetation before the narrator
  recognizes the moving source of death.

##### Variation and counterexamples

The local biological encounter and the catastrophe differ in scale, tempo,
and retrospective framing. A targeted search of eligible development passages
found adjacent extensions of the same consequence-before-closure mechanism
and no genuine resistant development passage.

##### Quantitative context

Witness-scale sentence and passage distributions do not measure revelation
timing, explanatory closure, scene pressure, or the accumulation of observable
consequences. The two focal passages also come from events at different scales,
so their source-level metrics are not comparable suspense norms.

##### Control distinction

Held-out catastrophe retains delayed explanatory closure, but retrospective
framing weakens the delay in the biological threat; the adventure control
shares observation-before-recognition pacing.

##### Unresolved uncertainty

Advance framing, retrospective narration, event scale, and genre pressure vary
together. The evidence does not identify a Wells-specific suspense mechanism
or its prevalence.

### 9. Humour, irony, tonal shifts, and controlled awkwardness

#### Finding 1

##### Claim IDs

- `humour-through-social-friction`

##### Finding

In the two focal passages, humour arises from social mismatch: Bunting's
ceremonious response meets Cuss's panic, and a staged mustard reference is
recast as a personal slight.

##### Classification and confidence

Classification: `wells_characteristic_but_shared`
Confidence: `moderate`

##### Jurisdiction

Validated, cross-work comparison of comic friction in *The Invisible
Man* and humour in *Certain Personal Matters*. The Tier 3 essay is a bounded
nonfiction comparison and does not provide fictional continuity or direct
fictional voice authority.

##### Evidence

- `the-invisible-man:iv:156:bea3b86ad1a8` (Tier 2) places Bunting's repeated,
  ceremonious formula against Cuss's agitated report.
- `certain-personal-matters:the-pleasure-of-quarrelling:359:58b49bd92afb`
  (Tier 3) stages a mismatch between ordinary conversation and a contrived
  interpretation of it.

##### Variation and counterexamples

`certain-personal-matters:the-pleasure-of-quarrelling:353:5d0691c29fcf` is a
genuine limiting variation: its mock-scientific hygienic and physiological
case for quarrelling uses a different comic mechanism. The focal comparison
therefore covers two instances of social mismatch, not humour across either
witness as a whole.

##### Quantitative context

Quotation, dialogue, sentence, and punctuation metrics do not identify humour,
social mismatch, irony, or the contribution of narratorial framing. The
quoted-span values are especially unsuitable for comparing a fictional
exchange with staged speech in a nonfiction essay, so no numerical norm is
drawn from them.

##### Control distinction

Held-out fiction and essay passages repeat humour through social mismatch, but
the control also makes incompatible assumptions comic through polite friction.

##### Unresolved uncertainty

Social mismatch interacts with narratorial irony, analogy, and character
premise. One control cannot establish period prevalence or isolate the
mechanism from adjacent comic devices.

### 10. Transitions, compression, reflection, and ending logic

#### Finding 1

##### Claim IDs

- `short-form-evidentiary-closure`

##### Finding

In the sampled frame and short-form endings, the available evidentiary record
closes while the event's larger implications remain unresolved.

##### Classification and confidence

Classification: `role_specific`
Confidence: `moderate`

##### Jurisdiction

Validated, cross-work finding for frame architecture in *The Time
Machine* and short-form architecture in *The Sea Raiders* and *The Crystal
Egg*. *The Time Machine* alone has continuity authority; the two selected
stories are independent Tier 2 short-form comparisons.

##### Evidence

- `the-time-machine:epilogue:308:6c18eecffc7e` (Tier 1) closes the available
  account by narrowing what can be claimed while leaving disappearance and
  future history unresolved.
- `selected-short-fiction/the-sea-raiders:iii:41:670a0256413b` (Tier 2) ends a
  dated record of attacks while leaving future biological threat unsettled.
- `selected-short-fiction/the-crystal-egg:the-crystal-egg:51:025f5af2df0e`
  (Tier 2) ends the object's traceable custody while leaving its observer-world
  implications unresolved.

##### Variation and counterexamples

The three passages close different records: reported testimony, dated attacks,
and object custody. A targeted development search found these differences but
no genuine resistant development passage; the former *Crystal Egg* variation
was reclassified as support because it preserves the bounded relation between
ended record and unresolved implication.

##### Quantitative context

Counts of sections, passages, sentences, and transition markers do not measure
whether an evidentiary record has closed or whether larger implications remain
unresolved. The sources also differ greatly in form and length, so their
witness-level metrics are sampling context rather than ending norms.

##### Control distinction

The held-out short story preserves unresolved implications, while the
held-out Time Machine sample confirms only local testimony closure. The
journey control instead settles its principal outcomes, a local contrast
rather than proof of Wells distinctiveness.

##### Unresolved uncertainty

The terminal *Time Machine* frame is absent from the held-out window, and only
one journey-architecture control was tested. Wider ending practice and
corpus-level distinctiveness therefore remain unresolved.
